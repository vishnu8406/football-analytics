# Player Discipline Analysis

## Objective
This analysis evaluates player discipline throughout the season by measuring fouls committed and cards received. The goal is to identify players whose aggressive style resulted in frequent disciplinary actions and understand which players posed the greatest disciplinary risk to their teams.

---

## Metrics Used

| Metric | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Matches Played | Number of matches participated in |
| Total Fouls | Total fouls committed |
| Yellow Cards | Yellow cards received |
| Red Cards | Direct red cards received |
| Second Yellow Cards | Red cards resulting from a second yellow |
| Total Cards | Sum of all cards received |
| Fouls Per Match | Average fouls committed per match |
| Cards Per Match | Average cards received per match |

---

## Key Findings

### Most Booked Players

Rubén Salvador Pérez Del Mármol led the league with **17 yellow cards**, making him the most frequently sanctioned player of the season.

Other heavily booked players included:

- José Luis García del Pozo — 16 yellow cards
- Gonzalo Escalante — 15 yellow cards
- Daniel García Carrillo — 15 yellow cards
- Pedro Pablo Hernández — 15 yellow cards
- Asier Illarramendi Andonegi — 15 yellow cards

These players consistently operated close to disciplinary limits throughout the season.

---

### Highest Fouling Players

Several players committed fouls at exceptionally high rates:

| Player | Fouls | Fouls Per Match |
|----------|--------|----------------|
| Gonzalo Escalante | 46 | 1.35 |
| José Luis García del Pozo | 33 | 0.97 |
| Rubén Salvador Pérez Del Mármol | 32 | 1.03 |
| Hernán Arsenio Pérez González | 34 | 1.06 |
| Papa Kouly Diop | 33 | 1.10 |

Gonzalo Escalante stood out as the most aggressive player, averaging more than one foul per match.

---

### Red Card Incidents

Direct red cards were relatively uncommon.

Players receiving red cards included:

- Ignacio Cases Mora
- Diego Javier Llorente Ríos
- Filipe Luís Kasmirski
- Simão Mate
- Gustavo Daniel Cabral Cáceres
- Jonathan Castro Otto
- Javier Alejandro Mascherano

Most disciplinary issues were managed through yellow cards rather than direct dismissals.

---

### Cards Per Match Leaders

The highest card accumulation rates were recorded by:

| Player | Cards Per Match |
|----------|---------------|
| Rubén Salvador Pérez Del Mármol | 0.55 |
| Mubarak Wakaso | 0.52 |
| José Luis García del Pozo | 0.47 |
| Papa Kouly Diop | 0.47 |
| Ignacio Cases Mora | 0.46 |

These players received a booking approximately every two matches.

---

### Defensive Midfield Influence

A significant number of highly booked players occupied:

- Defensive Midfield positions
- Central Midfield positions
- Central Defence positions

This suggests that tactical fouling, ball recovery attempts, and defensive duels contributed heavily to disciplinary records.

---

## Notable Observations

### Aggressive but Effective

Gonzalo Escalante:

- Highest foul count in the league (46)
- 15 yellow cards
- Maintained a starting role despite disciplinary risk

This indicates a highly aggressive defensive playing style.

### High Discipline Risk

Rubén Salvador Pérez Del Mármol:

- League-high 17 yellow cards
- Over 1 foul per match
- Highest cards-per-match rate among regular starters

### Controlled Aggression

Players such as Gerard Piqué Bernabéu accumulated many yellow cards despite relatively low foul totals, suggesting tactical fouls in critical situations rather than persistent infringement.

---

## Conclusion

The discipline analysis reveals that:

- Defensive and midfield players accumulated the majority of bookings.
- Yellow cards were significantly more common than red cards.
- Gonzalo Escalante was the league's most aggressive fouler.
- Rubén Salvador Pérez Del Mármol was the most frequently booked player.
- Most disciplinary incidents were the result of persistent fouling rather than serious misconduct.

Overall, disciplinary records closely aligned with defensive responsibilities and physical playing styles, highlighting the trade-off between defensive effectiveness and disciplinary risk.
