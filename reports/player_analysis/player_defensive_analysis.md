# Player Defensive Analysis

## Objective

This analysis evaluates the defensive performance of players throughout the season. Defensive contributions are measured using tackles, interceptions, recoveries, clearances, and blocks. The goal is to identify the league's most impactful defenders and defensive midfielders based on their ability to disrupt opposition attacks and regain possession.

---

## Metrics Used

| Metric | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Matches Played | Total matches played |
| Tackles Won | Successful tackles won |
| Interceptions | Opponent passes intercepted |
| Recoveries | Ball recoveries |
| Clearances | Defensive clearances |
| Blocks | Shots or passes blocked |
| Total Defensive Actions | Sum of all defensive metrics |
| Defensive Actions Per Match | Average defensive actions per match |

---

## Key Findings

### Most Active Defenders

The league's highest defensive contributors were primarily central defenders responsible for protecting the penalty area.

| Player | Defensive Actions |

| Luis Hernández Rodríguez | 358 |
| Bruno González Cabrera | 324 |
| Zouhair Feddal | 300 |
| Sidnei Rechel da Silva Júnior | 298 |
| Shkodran Mustafi | 287 |

## Positional Insights

### Central Defenders

The majority of the league leaders were center-backs, reflecting their responsibilities for:

- Clearing dangerous situations
- Winning aerial duels
- Intercepting passes
- Blocking shots

Notable examples:

- Luis Hernández Rodríguez
- Bruno González Cabrera
- Shkodran Mustafi
- Zouhair Feddal
- Sidnei Rechel da Silva Júnior

---

### Defensive Midfielders

Several midfielders excelled through interceptions, tackles, and recoveries rather than clearances.

Key examples:

- Gonzalo Escalante
- Pedro Mosquera Parada
- Asier Illarramendi Andonegi
- Grzegorz Krychowiak
- Papa Kouly Diop

These players served as the first defensive shield ahead of the back line.

---

### Fullbacks

Modern fullbacks contributed significantly through interceptions and recoveries.

Top performers included:

- Ander Capa Rodríguez
- Hugo Mallo Novegil
- Filipe Luís Kasmirski
- Jonathan Castro Otto
- Mikel Balenziaga Oruesagasti

Their defensive contributions were balanced between defending and supporting attacking phases.

---

## Notable Observations

### Defensive Workhorse

Luis Hernández Rodríguez led the league in:

- Total Defensive Actions
- Interceptions
- Defensive Actions Per Match

His performance represents one of the most complete defensive seasons in the dataset.

### Aggressive Ball Winner

Gonzalo Escalante recorded:

- League-high 45 tackles won
- 189 total defensive actions

His profile reflects a highly active ball-winning midfielder.

### Elite Defensive Consistency

Bruno González Cabrera averaged over 9.5 defensive actions per match while accumulating more than 220 clearances, demonstrating exceptional consistency throughout the season.

---

## Conclusion

The defensive analysis reveals that:

- Central defenders dominate total defensive action rankings.
- Defensive midfielders lead tackle and recovery metrics.
- Luis Hernández Rodríguez was the most productive defender overall.
- Gonzalo Escalante was the league's premier ball-winning midfielder.
- Defensive success is achieved through different styles, including tackling, intercepting, clearing, and recovering possession.

Overall, the results highlight the players who provided the strongest defensive foundations for their teams throughout the season.
