# Player Attacking Summary Analysis
## La Liga 2015–16 Season

---

## Overview

This report evaluates the attacking performance of players during the 2015–16 La Liga season using key offensive and passing metrics derived from StatsBomb event data.

The objective of this analysis is to identify the league's most productive attackers, creators, and possession contributors by examining goals, assists, shooting activity, and passing involvement.

---

## Metrics Included

| Metric | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Matches Played | Total appearances during the season |
| Goals | Goals scored by the player |
| Assists | Final pass leading directly to a goal |
| Shots | Total shot attempts |
| Passes Attempted | Total passes attempted |
| Passes Completed | Successful passes completed |
| Pass Accuracy (%) | Completed passes divided by attempted passes |

---

## Analysis Scope

This report focuses primarily on attacking output and ball distribution.

Players are ranked based on offensive production, particularly goals scored.

The report does not include:

- Defensive contributions
- Ball recoveries
- Tackles
- Interceptions
- Pressures
- Goalkeeping statistics
- Advanced metrics such as xG and xA

As a result, this analysis should be interpreted as an **attacking performance report rather than a complete player performance ranking**.

# Tactical Observations

## Barcelona

The data reveals a possession-dominant attacking system.

Key characteristics:

- High passing involvement from attackers.
- Significant chance creation distributed across multiple players.
- Strong balance between scoring and creativity.

---

## Real Madrid

The attack centered around direct offensive production.

Key characteristics:

- Highest shot volume.
- Heavy reliance on Ronaldo's finishing.
- Strong secondary support from Benzema and Bale.

---

## Atlético Madrid

The attack was more concentrated.

Key characteristics:

- Lower overall attacking volume.
- High dependence on Griezmann.
- Greater emphasis on defensive organization.

---

# Limitations

This report focuses on attacking statistics.

The following player qualities are not measured:

- Defensive actions
- Ball recoveries
- Tackles
- Pressures
- Interceptions
- Goalkeeping contributions
- Expected Goals (xG)
- Expected Assists (xA)

Therefore, players with strong defensive or creative contributions may be undervalued in this ranking.

---

# Conclusion

The analysis highlights the dominance of Barcelona's attacking unit and the exceptional individual performances delivered throughout the season.

### Major Findings

- Luis Suárez was the league's most productive attacker.
- Cristiano Ronaldo generated the highest shooting volume.
- Lionel Messi remained the most complete offensive player.
- Barcelona's MSN trio dominated attacking production.
- Jonathan Viera and Daniel Parejo emerged as elite creators outside the title contenders.
- Gareth Bale and Karim Benzema delivered outstanding efficiency when available.
