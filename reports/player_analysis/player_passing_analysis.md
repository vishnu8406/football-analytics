# Player Passing Analysis

## Objective

This analysis evaluates player passing performance across the season, focusing on passing volume, accuracy, creativity, and distribution effectiveness. The goal is to identify elite playmakers, possession controllers, and players who contribute most to chance creation.

---

# Metrics Used

| Metric | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Matches Played | Number of matches participated in |
| Passes Attempted | Total passes attempted |
| Passes Completed | Successful passes completed |
| Pass Accuracy (%) | Completed Passes ÷ Attempted Passes × 100 |
| Passes per Match | Average passes attempted per match |
| Key Passes | Passes directly leading to a shot |
| Assists | Passes directly leading to a goal |
| Long Passes | Passes longer than 30 units |

---

# Overall Findings

## 1. Neymar Was the League's Most Creative Passer

Neymar produced:

- 1,970 passes attempted
- 1,532 completed passes
- 77.77% pass accuracy
- 94 key passes
- 12 assists

No player created more shooting opportunities than Neymar, making him the league's most influential chance creator.

---

## 2. Messi Combined Creativity with Efficiency

Lionel Messi recorded:

| Metric | Value |
|----------|---------|
| Passes Attempted | 1,926 |
| Passes Completed | 1,544 |
| Pass Accuracy | 80.17% |
| Key Passes | 74 |
| Assists | 15 |

Messi ranked among the league leaders in both assists and key passes while maintaining excellent passing efficiency.

---

## 3. Koke Was the Best Midfield Creator

Jorge Resurrección "Koke" produced:

- 2,272 passes attempted
- 1,743 completed
- 78 key passes
- 14 assists
- 387 long passes

Koke combined creativity with ball progression, making him one of the most complete midfield playmakers.

# Tactical Observations

## Creative Attackers

Examples:

- Neymar
- Messi
- Bale
- Cristiano Ronaldo
- Luis Suárez

Characteristics:

- High key-pass numbers
- Strong assist production
- Lower passing volume than midfielders
- Greater involvement in final-third actions

---

## Possession Controllers

Examples:

- Kroos
- Modrić
- Koke
- Parejo
- Trashorras

Characteristics:

- High passing volume
- High pass accuracy
- Significant long-pass contribution

# Conclusion

The passing analysis highlights two dominant player profiles within the league:

### Creative Attackers
- Neymar
- Messi
- Suárez
- Bale

These players generated the majority of goal-scoring opportunities through key passes and assists.

### Possession Controllers
- Kroos
- Modrić
- Koke
- Parejo
- Trashorras

These players dictated tempo, maintained possession, and distributed the ball across all phases of play.

Barcelona's attacking trio emerged as the most productive creative unit, while Real Madrid's midfield core demonstrated the strongest combination of passing accuracy, distribution, and possession control.
