# Player Expected Goals (xG) Analysis

## Objective

This analysis evaluates player shooting quality and finishing efficiency using Expected Goals (xG).

Unlike traditional goal statistics, xG measures the probability of a shot becoming a goal based on factors such as shot location, angle, distance, and shooting situation.

The objective is to identify:

- Players generating the highest quality chances
- Clinical finishers who outperform xG
- Wasteful finishers who underperform xG
- Consistent goal threats across the season

---

## Metrics Used

### Goals
Total goals scored by the player.

### Total Shots
Total number of shots attempted.

### Total xG
Sum of expected goals from all shots.

### xG per Match
Average expected goals generated per match.

### Average xG per Shot
Average quality of each shot taken.

### Goals Minus xG
Difference between actual goals scored and expected goals.

Formula:

Goals - xG

Interpretation:

- Positive Value → Player outperformed expectations
- Negative Value → Player underperformed expectations
- Zero → Finished exactly as expected

### Finishing Efficiency (%)

Formula:

(Goals / xG) × 100

Interpretation:

- 100% → Expected finishing
- Above 100% → Clinical finishing
- Below 100% → Wasteful finishing

# Key Findings

## Highest xG Producers

The players generating the highest quality chances were:

| Player | xG |
|----------|------|
| Cristiano Ronaldo | 31.97 |
| Luis Suárez | 27.65 |
| Lionel Messi | 21.26 |
| Neymar | 20.47 |
| Aritz Aduriz | 18.72 |

These players consistently occupied dangerous attacking positions and generated high-value opportunities throughout the season.

---

## Best Finishers (Goals - xG)

Players who scored significantly more goals than expected:

| Player | Goals - xG |
|----------|-----------|
| Luis Suárez | +12.35 |
| Gareth Bale | +10.01 |
| Karim Benzema | +6.59 |
| Antoine Griezmann | +6.03 |
| Lionel Messi | +4.74 |

Luis Suárez was the most clinical finisher in the league, scoring more than twelve goals above expectation.

Gareth Bale's performance was particularly remarkable, massively outperforming his expected goal total.

---

## Conclusion

The xG analysis highlights that goal totals alone do not fully describe attacking performance.

Cristiano Ronaldo generated the highest expected goals, indicating he consistently received high-quality scoring opportunities.

Luis Suárez emerged as the most clinical finisher, significantly outperforming his expected goals and leading the league in goals scored.

Gareth Bale produced the highest finishing efficiency, converting opportunities at more than twice the expected rate.

Conversely, players such as Álvaro Vázquez and Carlos Vela generated good chances but failed to convert them efficiently.

Overall, xG provides a more complete evaluation of attacking effectiveness by separating chance creation from finishing ability.
